HIPAA Compliance

Our unwavering commitment to the privacy of your Protected Health Information (PHI).

What is HIPAA?

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is a federal law that required the creation of national standards to protect sensitive patient health information from being disclosed without the patient’s consent or knowledge. At Docipher, we embrace these standards not just as a legal requirement, but as a moral imperative to protect our users.

Physical & Technical Safeguards

End-to-End Encryption

All PHI transmitted between your browser, our servers, and our LLM partners is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3. Your data is protected from interception in transit.

Encryption at Rest

All data stored in our databases, document storage buckets, and backups is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. We utilize advanced key management systems (KMS).

Access Controls

We enforce strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC). Docipher engineers do not have direct access to your PHI unless explicitly required for support and authorized by you.

Audit Logging

Comprehensive audit trails track all system access, data modifications, and API calls. Any unauthorized access attempts are immediately flagged and investigated.

Administrative Safeguards

  • Security Personnel: We have a designated Security Officer responsible for the development and implementation of the security policies and procedures required by HIPAA.
  • Workforce Training: Every Docipher employee undergoes mandatory, rigorous HIPAA compliance and data security training upon hire, and annually thereafter.
  • Incident Response Plan: We maintain a robust Incident Response Plan to address and mitigate any potential security incidents or data breaches, including rapid notification procedures as mandated by the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule.

Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)

We do not share your PHI with any third-party vendor without executing a formal Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

Crucially, this applies to the AI Language Models that power Docipher. We use enterprise-grade APIs from our LLM providers (such as Google Cloud Vertex AI or OpenAI Enterprise) backed by BAAs. This guarantees that:

  • Your data is NOT used to train their public or foundation models.
  • Your data is processed securely and deleted from their temporary processing memory according to strict retention policies (often zero-data retention).

If you require a BAA for your clinic or enterprise, please contact compliance@docipher.com.
