### 1️⃣ Fever Query

**User**: "I am feeling fever today"

**Expected Response**:
```
**🌡️ Fever - Understanding and Management**

**Overview:**
Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, usually a sign that your body 
is fighting an infection. Normal body temperature is around 98.6°F (37°C). 
A temperature above 100.4°F (38°C) is generally considered a fever.

**🌿 Home Remedies:**
- **Rest**: Get plenty of sleep (7-8 hours minimum)
- **Hydration**: Drink 8-10 glasses of water, herbal teas, or clear soups
- **Cool Compress**: Apply a cool, damp cloth to forehead and wrists
- **Lukewarm Bath**: Take a lukewarm (not cold) bath to reduce temperature
- **Light Clothing**: Wear light, breathable clothing
- **Room Temperature**: Keep room comfortably cool
- **Herbal Teas**: Ginger tea, chamomile, or peppermint tea
- **Nutrition**: Light, easily digestible foods (khichdi, soup)

**💊 Common Medicines:**
- **Paracetamol/Acetaminophen**: 500mg-1000mg every 4-6 hours (max 4g/day)
- **Ibuprofen**: 400mg every 6-8 hours (take with food)
- **Aspirin**: 325mg-650mg every 4 hours (adults only, not for children)

Note: These are over-the-counter medicines. Take as directed on package.

**🚨 When to See a Doctor:**
- Temperature above 103°F (39.4°C)
- Fever lasting more than 3 days
- Severe headache or neck stiffness
- Difficulty breathing or chest pain
- Persistent vomiting or diarrhea
- Confusion or altered mental state
- Rash appearing with fever
- Fever in infants under 3 months

⚠️ MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: This information is for educational purposes only 
and does not replace professional medical advice. Please consult a qualified 
healthcare professional or doctor for proper diagnosis and treatment. 
Do not self-medicate without professional guidance.
```

### 3️⃣ Cold and Cough

**User**: "I have cold and cough. What should I do?"

**Expected Response**:
```
**🤧 Cold & Cough Management**

**Overview:**
Common cold is a viral infection of the upper respiratory tract. It typically 
lasts 7-10 days and includes symptoms like runny nose, sore throat, cough, 
and congestion.

**🌿 Home Remedies:**

*For Cold:*
- **Steam Inhalation**: 2-3 times daily (add eucalyptus oil)
- **Warm Liquids**: Herbal teas, warm water with honey and lemon
- **Saline Nasal Drops**: For nasal congestion
- **Rest**: Adequate sleep helps immune system
- **Vitamin C**: Citrus fruits, amla
- **Ginger Tea**: Natural anti-inflammatory

*For Cough:*
- **Honey**: 1-2 teaspoons before bed (not for infants under 1 year)
- **Turmeric Milk**: Traditional remedy with anti-inflammatory properties
- **Throat Lozenges**: Soothe throat irritation
- **Humidifier**: Add moisture to air

**💊 Common Medicines:**

*For Cold:*
- **Cetirizine**: 10mg once daily (antihistamine)
- **Phenylephrine**: Nasal decongestant
- **Paracetamol**: For fever/body ache

*For Cough:*
- **Dextromethorphan**: For dry cough (10-20mg every 4 hours)
- **Guaifenesin**: For productive cough (expectorant)
- **Cough Syrups**: As per age-appropriate dosing

**Preventive Tips:**
- Wash hands frequently
- Avoid touching face
- Stay away from sick people
- Get adequate sleep
- Maintain good nutrition

**🚨 When to See a Doctor:**
- Symptoms lasting more than 10 days
- High fever (above 101.3°F)
- Severe sore throat or difficulty swallowing
- Difficulty breathing or wheezing
- Coughing up blood or green/yellow phlegm
- Ear pain or severe headache

⚠️ MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: This information is for educational purposes only 
and does not replace professional medical advice. Please consult a qualified 
healthcare professional for proper diagnosis and treatment.
```

### 5️⃣ Stomach Ache

**User**: "My stomach is hurting"

**Expected Response**:
```
**🤰 Stomach Pain - Causes and Relief**

**Overview:**
Stomach pain can result from various causes including indigestion, gas, 
acidity, food poisoning, or menstrual cramps. Most cases resolve with 
simple home care.

**🌿 Home Remedies:**
- **Ginger**: Fresh ginger tea or ginger water
- **Peppermint**: Peppermint tea for digestion
- **Warm Water**: Sip slowly throughout the day
- **BRAT Diet**: Banana, Rice, Applesauce, Toast
- **Heating Pad**: Apply to abdomen for comfort
- **Fennel Seeds**: Chew after meals
- **Chamomile Tea**: Soothing and anti-inflammatory
- **Avoid**: Spicy, oily, heavy foods

**💊 Common Medicines:**
- **Antacids**: Gelusil, Digene (for acidity)
- **Omeprazole**: 20mg (for severe acidity)
- **Simethicone**: For gas and bloating
- **Dicyclomine**: For abdominal cramps (antispasmodic)
- **Activated Charcoal**: For food poisoning

**🚨 When to See a Doctor IMMEDIATELY:**
- Severe, persistent pain
- Pain with fever or vomiting
- Blood in vomit or stool
- Inability to pass stool or gas
- Pregnant and experiencing pain
- Pain radiating to chest, neck, or shoulder
- Rigid, hard abdomen

⚠️ MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: Stomach pain can have serious causes. 
This information is for mild, occasional discomfort only. 
Please consult a healthcare professional for persistent or severe pain.
```
